# Recipes

Step-by-step setups that build on what the app sends. Each one lists what it needs first, and the YAML is ready to paste after you replace the placeholders in angle brackets.

| Recipe | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Your alarm and your night, together](alarm-and-sleep.md) | A bedtime reminder based on your next alarm and your sleep goal, a week of sleep next to that goal, and how long before your alarm you wake up. Uses the Home Assistant companion app's next alarm sensor |
| [Your scale into Samsung Health or Google Health](scale-to-health-connect.md) | A Xiaomi, Renpho, Eufy or Withings scale, or a scale's cloud account, from Home Assistant into Health Connect, with the templates each route needs |
| [Screen time limits in Home Assistant](screen-time-limits.md) | A notification when today's screen time or one app passes a limit, and a flag for phone use late at night |
| [Ask your own LLM about your health data](ask-your-own-llm.md) | A language model on your own hardware that answers questions about your history, through a read-only connection to Home Assistant or life-dashboard-stack |

What the app sends, and every sensor it creates, is in [features.md](../features.md). Setup of the app itself is in [usage.md](../usage.md).
