Life Dashboard Companion syncs your Health Connect and Screen Time data into Home Assistant, or to your own server via webhooks. Built for self-hosted dashboards and quantified-self projects that refuse third-party clouds.

Your data goes to YOUR endpoints and nowhere else. The app contains no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.

Home Assistant:
- Recommended: the Life Dashboard integration, installed through HACS. Pair by scanning the QR code it shows; no broker and no YAML. Every synced day lands in Home Assistant's long-term statistics on its own date, so a backfill becomes a year of history
- Receive: a scale or blood pressure monitor paired with Home Assistant can write its measurements into Health Connect through the integration (weight, height, body composition, blood pressure), per type and only when you switch it on
- Alternative: MQTT Discovery, for a setup that already runs a broker and only needs the latest values. 24 of the 33 data types plus screen time appear as sensors under one device; workouts, meals, mindfulness and cycle tracking are not published over MQTT. States are retained, with optional TLS and username/password authentication

Health Connect:
- 33 data types: steps, sleep with stages, heart rate, HRV, blood pressure, blood glucose, VO2 max, skin temperature, full cycle tracking, and more
- Per-record source app and stable record id, so multi-source setups (phone plus watch) can deduplicate server-side
- Incremental sync with per-type watermarks; resilient against malformed records from source apps

Screen Time:
- App usage statistics with a configurable day boundary (night-owl friendly), sent to Home Assistant, your webhooks and MQTT

Webhooks:
- Multiple URLs, custom headers, and optional HMAC-SHA256 payload signing so your server can verify the sender
- Automatic retries with exponential backoff, plus an outbox that holds undelivered syncs for up to a week while your server is down
- Detailed delivery logs with payload inspection in the app

Automation:
- Home screen widget and Quick Settings tile
- Tasker/MacroDroid trigger via broadcast intent
- Failure notifications with a configurable threshold

Also: settings backup and restore (password-encrypted when secrets are included), historical backfill of up to a year, and an English, Dutch and German interface.

Supply-chain transparency: releases are built by public CI, signed, and carry verifiable sigstore build provenance. The project holds an OpenSSF Best Practices badge.

Requires the Google Health Connect app. An iOS companion app with a compatible payload is available for mixed households; it sends to webhooks and MQTT, and does not pair with the Home Assistant integration yet.
